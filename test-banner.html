Historical Hong Kong: Maritime Lens

Exploring Hong Kong's rich maritime history through interactive maps, archival documents, and digital storytelling

Welcome to Historical Hong Kong

This project aims to document and visualize Hong Kong's maritime history through an interactive digital platform. By combining historical maps, archival research, and modern web technologies, we create an engaging experience for exploring the city's past.

Hong Kong's development has always been intrinsically linked to the sea. From early fishing communities to international trading port, the waters around Hong Kong have witnessed centuries of human activity, cultural exchange, and economic transformation.

About This Project

Using digital humanities methodologies, we georeference historical maps, digitize archival documents, and create interactive visualizations that make Hong Kong's maritime history accessible to researchers, students, and the general public.
